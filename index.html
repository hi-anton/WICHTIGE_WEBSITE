About me

I'm a passionate developer based in Germany dedicated to creating meaningful digital experiences. With expertise spanning web development, user interface design, and full-stack applications, I focus on building solutions that are functional, elegant and intuitive. My journey has been driven by a commitment to continuous learning and staying current with emerging technologies. I believe in the power of clean code, thoughtful design, and collaborative teamwork to solve complex problems and deliver exceptional results.

Accessibility

I'm committed to creating web experiences that are accessible to everyone, regardless of their abilities or device. This means designing with semantic HTML, ensuring proper color contrast, supporting keyboard navigation, and implementing ARIA labels where needed. Accessibility isn't an afterthought—it's a core principle embedded in my development process from the start. By prioritizing inclusivity, I ensure that my applications are usable by all users, which ultimately creates better experiences for everyone.

Values

At the heart of my work are core values that guide every decision and interaction. I value transparency in communication, quality in execution, and respect for my collaborators and end-users. I believe in writing maintainable code that others can understand and build upon, fostering an environment of knowledge-sharing and continuous improvement. Integrity, creativity, and a user-centric approach form the foundation of my professional philosophy.
